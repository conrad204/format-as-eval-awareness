# Frozen analysis plan v2 (proposedchanges.md item 18)

**Status: FROZEN as of 2026-08-16.** This plan and its executable,
`scripts/confirmation_analysis.py`, are hash-stamped in
`docs/analysis_plan_v2.freeze.json`. Amendments are allowed only as dated
addenda in that stamp file with a stated reason; silent edits invalidate the
confirmation claim. Everything run before the single confirmation execution
is development/exploratory by definition, including all analyses of the v2
pilot dataset.

## 1. Estimand and claim language (binds item 4)

- **Primary estimand:** linear decodability of *stated response purpose*
  (evaluation vs deployment) from the last-prompt-token residual stream,
  generalizing across cue vocabulary the probe has never seen. This is not
  "evaluation awareness" and must not be reported as such.
- **Secondary estimands:** decodability from *indirect* (context-implied)
  purpose cues; transfer to *self-referential* (second-person) cues; and the
  *format-induced drift* of purpose-neutral prompts along the purpose
  direction (the one endpoint that speaks, weakly, to latent evaluation
  awareness).

## 2. Data

- **Development (spent):** the frozen 288-item v1 dataset; the v2 pilot
  (`rendered_items_v2_pilot.jsonl`, 1584 items, 72 blocks); all their
  activations and sweeps. Used freely to pick layers and sanity-check the
  design. Never reported as confirmation.
- **Confirmation bank (to be generated, then sealed):**
  1. Fresh payload blocks: new topics plus refills to quota, generated by a
     model lineage different from grok-4.3, validated per the
     items-13/14/15 pipeline (deterministic solvers where the task family
     allows, quota enforcement after validation, logged rejections).
  2. Fresh frame catalog: same structure as v2 (5 folds × 10 pairs, same
     composition), authored by a **different model lineage than the v2 dev
     catalog** (dev was authored by Claude; the confirmation catalog must
     not be), passing `lint-frames-v2` **plus cross-catalog disjointness**:
     its declared stems must not appear among v1 or v2-dev cue vocabulary.
  3. Balance requirements: answer-key letters uniform (item 17b);
     locus/style composition identical to v2 by construction.
  4. Rendered by `render-v2` unchanged; manifest hash policy enforced;
     the bank is extracted once per model and then not edited.

## 3. Frozen probe recipe

- Probe: `StandardScaler` + `LogisticRegression(C=0.1, max_iter=3000,
  random_state=0)` on the pre-norm residual stream at the last prompt token
  (extraction settings identical to `provenance/extraction_config_*.json`).
- Splits: leave-one-vocabulary-fold-out on the stated third-person stratum
  (`purpose_family_id` = fold). Indirect, second-person, and no-cue items are
  scored only by models whose training excluded their fold (no-cue items,
  having no fold, are scored by the mean of the five held-fold models).
- **Layer selection rule:** for each model, the confirmation layer is
  `argmax` of decorrelated stated held-fold AUC **on the v2 pilot**. The
  chosen layer per model must be written into the freeze stamp as an
  addendum *before* confirmation extraction. The confirmation report is at
  that layer only; a full layer sweep may appear in an appendix labeled
  exploratory.

## 4. Endpoints (as implemented in `confirmation_analysis.py`)

| id | definition | success criterion |
|----|------------|-------------------|
| E1 | stated held-fold AUC; the same-split cue-sentence TF-IDF n-gram AUC (permanent control, item 2); their difference | **primary:** difference ≥ 0.05 with 95% CI excluding 0 |
| E2 | E1 AUC by cue locus (noun-only / verb-only / both) | noun-only CI lower bound > 0.5 (item 3's question) |
| E3 | cross-format transfer b2c and c2b within stated | report with CIs |
| E4 | indirect-cue AUC under stated-trained held-fold probes; indirect-only CV | CI lower bound > 0.5 upgrades the estimand to "implied purpose" |
| E5 | second-person AUC under third-person-trained probes (overall and by noun/context sub-locus); second-only CV | report; transfer vs own-CV gap is the "same representation?" readout (item 6) |
| E6 | no-cue format shift: standardized mean difference (benchmark − casual) of no-cue items projected on the purpose direction, plus the corresponding AUC | estimation endpoint, two-sided, no gate (item 5) |
| E7 | payload holdout (5 deterministic round-robin topic groups), frames seen | report with CIs |
| E8 | doubly held out: fold × topic-group | report with CIs; the strictest generalization number |

Also reported, per item 12: per-fold stated AUCs with clustered CIs.

## 5. Statistics

Cluster bootstrap over `payload_block_id` (blocks are the independent unit;
resampling items would understate uncertainty ~4-fold), B = 10,000, seed 0,
percentile 95% CIs, degenerate resamples (single-class) dropped and counted.
No multiplicity correction: E1 is the sole gated primary endpoint; all other
endpoints are reported as estimates with CIs regardless of outcome.

## 6. One-shot rule

`confirmation_analysis.py --confirmatory` is executed **once** per model on
the sealed bank, at the pre-registered layer, and its JSON output is the
reported result — including any failures. If a code defect forces a rerun,
the stamp file gains an addendum stating what changed and why before the
rerun; the first output is retained alongside. Any further analysis of the
bank is labeled exploratory and cannot promote itself to confirmation.

## 7. Reporting requirements

Every report of E1 must show the n-gram control next to the probe AUC
(item 2). Strata results are reported with their block counts. Claims use
the estimand language of §1. If E1 fails, the headline conclusion is that
the v1 result did not survive vocabulary-disjoint confirmation — that
negative is publishable and must not be softened by post-hoc splits.
