**Figure.** `binding_v1`: response purpose recast as a factorial relational
binding. Every prompt names two nonce routes and describes BOTH use roles (one
measurement role whose consequences are discarded, one operational role whose
consequences persist); the label is the interaction `p = m * s` of the
role-to-route assignment with the route the current response belongs to. All
4096 items are exactly balanced over mapping x selected route x format, and every
text and metadata leakage baseline sits at exactly 0.500 under
leave-one-relational-family-out CV. Inference unit is the relational-template
family (n = 8); lines and points are family means.

**A.** The persisted explicit-purpose probes (refit only from the original
explicitly stated purpose rows, never from `binding_v1`) applied without
retraining. Shaded band is the family bootstrap 95% CI. The frozen explicit
direction sits at chance at every 8B depth and at the pre-specified layer 18 of
both 70B models, but rises to about 0.57-0.62 family-mean AUC across a broad
band in the deeper half of both 70B networks (7/8 families, CIs excluding 0.5).
Only layer 18 was pre-registered; that deeper band was identified post hoc.

**B.** Probes trained on `binding_v1` with leave-one-entire-family-out CV
(same recipe as the paper: train-only scaler, `LogisticRegression(C=0.1)`).
Solid = joint-format AUC, dotted / open markers = mean cross-format AUC
(benchmark->casual and casual->benchmark). The bound purpose is clearly
decodable from held-out relational framings, and the original
format-conditioned transfer deficit reappears (gap positive in 8/8 families in
all three checkpoints).

**C.** Matched factorial contrast norms for Llama-3.1-8B on a log axis. The
bound-purpose interaction `|P|` grows with depth while the additive
route-assignment and selected-route main effects `|M|`, `|S|` stay near zero;
the format main effect `|F|` dominates throughout. `cos(P, frozen explicit
probe)` is about +0.02 at all depths, which independently explains panel A.

All three checkpoints are shown at full layer resolution (8B: 32 layers; both
70B: 80 layers) on a relative-depth axis. Layer 18 was the pre-specified
validation layer inherited from the earlier explicit study and was fixed before
any `binding_v1` result was seen; the full-depth sweeps were run afterwards. Design orthogonality of the
factorial coding does not imply geometric orthogonality of the learned vectors,
and this is a frozen-representation probing result, not a behavioral or causal
intervention. Generated by `scripts/binding_v1_report.py`; full method,
statistics, and claim boundaries in `docs/binding_v1_results.md`.
