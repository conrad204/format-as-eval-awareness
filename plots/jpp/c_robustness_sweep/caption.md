**Figure.** Purpose AUC and cross-format transfer gap are stable across a 1000x range
of the logistic C penalty.

The balanced 2x2 purpose probe (StandardScaler + LogisticRegression, 5-fold held-out
purpose-family cross-validation) was refit at C in {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10} -- three orders
of magnitude around the pinned C=0.1 operating point -- at the pinned min-gap, final,
and early-layer spike depths, for all three checkpoints. Neither the purpose-AUC
magnitude (top row) nor the cross-format transfer gap G = AUC_decorrelated -
mean(AUC_b2c, AUC_c2b) (bottom row) shows meaningful sensitivity to C at any depth or
checkpoint: AUC varies by at most 2.3 points and gap by at most 3.5 points across the
full sweep, at any single depth. This rules out "the purpose signal or the transfer
gap is a regularization artifact of the pinned C=0.1" as an explanation for either
quantity.
