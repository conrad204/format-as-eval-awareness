**Figure.** Full-layer depth trajectory of purpose decodability and cross-format
transfer gap, replacing the "spike/min_gap/final" 3-point convention used in earlier
figures.

That convention was found to be inconsistent across checkpoints on inspection:
`min_gap` sits at 23.8-26.2% relative depth for the two 70B checkpoints but 43.8% for
Llama-3.1-8B -- not a comparable "depth role" across models despite the shared label,
even though `spike` (5.0-9.4%) and `final` (trivially 100%) are reasonably consistent.
This figure plots the actual continuous trajectory against relative depth (0 = first
layer, 1 = last layer, making the 32-layer 8B and 80-layer 70B checkpoints directly
comparable), for both the raw (no erasure) probe and the same probe after
matched-format-PCA erasure at rank 64, at every layer.

Purpose AUC (left) rises sharply and plateaus near 1.0 from roughly 10-40% relative
depth, then declines slowly toward the final layer, in all three checkpoints; the raw
and rank-64-erased curves are nearly indistinguishable everywhere, not only at the
three depths previously tested -- the purpose/format geometric separation established
elsewhere in this results/ directory holds across the full depth range, not just at
hand-picked points. The transfer gap (right) shows a sharp early spike (~5-10% depth),
a genuine trough around 20-30% depth, and gradual late-layer regrowth toward the final
layer; erasure suppresses the gap substantially at nearly every depth, most dramatically
at the early spike. Small dots mark the old spike/min_gap/final reference layers for
continuity with earlier figures in this directory -- they are not the primary claim
here.

Source: results/layer_sweep_full320-v3_<model>.json (raw baseline, pinned, all
layers), results/full320_v3_erasure_full_layer_sweep.json (rank-64 erasure, all
layers, computed on the uahpc cluster via
scripts/full320_v3_erasure_full_layer_sweep.py -- 5-fold cross-fitted OOF scoring,
matched_pca method, single rank=64, no bootstrap in this pass). Generated by
generate.py in this directory (reads only pinned JSON, no activation extraction or
probe refitting in this script).
