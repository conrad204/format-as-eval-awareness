**Figure.** Purpose-direction energy inside the matched-format PCA subspace is far
below the exact high-dimensional chance level, across models and depths, with a
known rank-1 blind spot.

**Left:** observed E_purpose→format(r) = ||Q_format(r)^T w_purpose||^2 (solid lines,
one per model x depth) against the exact chance level r/d for a random unit vector
in the model's own ambient dimension d (dashed lines, one per model; d=4096 for
Llama-3.1-8B, d=8192 for both 70B checkpoints). Observed values sit at or below the
matching-color dashed chance line at essentially every point, with the closest
approach at rank 64 on Llama-3.1-70B's min-gap depth (orange square) -- the one cell
that fails significance in the right panel.

**Right:** count of the 9 model x depth cells reaching significance at each rank,
under two null models (full-space isotropic Beta(r/2,(d-r)/2); high-variance-subspace
conditional null, restricted to random subspaces drawn from within the empirical
top-64 PC subspace) and under nominal / Holm / Bonferroni correction (Holm/Bonferroni
applied within each null's own full 27-test family, not per-rank). Ranks 4 and 16 are
fully robust (9/9 under every correction, both nulls). Rank 1 is not: only 3/9
(full-space) or 5/9 (conditional) reach nominal significance, and only 0/9 or 1/9
survive family-wise correction -- an underpowered-at-tiny-magnitude effect, not a
sign reversal (no cell at rank 1 points the wrong direction). Rank 64 has no
conditional-null bar because a random rank-64 subspace of the 64-dim top-PC space is
the whole space, making that specific control undefined there.
