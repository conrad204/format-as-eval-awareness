# Purpose-by-format probe study: full320-v3 results summary (frozen 2026-08-17, analyzed 2026-08-18)

## Research question

When a linear probe on an LLM's activations distinguishes "evaluation" prompts from "deployment" prompts (so-called evaluation awareness), is it reading the model's representation of the prompt's *purpose*, or just its *surface format*? Prior work (Devbunova, "Is Evaluation Awareness Just Format Sensitivity?", ICBINB workshop @ ICLR 2026, arXiv:2603.19426) showed on Llama-3.1-8B that standard probes trained on the natural confound (benchmark-formatted eval prompts vs casual deployment prompts) track format — 94.5% of deployment prompts rewritten into benchmark format are misclassified as "evaluation" — but that training on a format-decorrelated 2×2 recovers correct classification. That paper's cells came from four different source distributions (MMLU/ARC/GSM8K, ShareGPT, MuTual first turns as an admitted proxy, DeepSeek-V3 rewrites), controlled only by length histogram-matching and a leakage audit, on one 8B model at 9 layers. The v3 study below re-tests the question with fully synthetic minimal-pair data that removes those confounds by construction, on three models at every layer.

## Dataset (full320-v3)

- 2×2 design: **intended_purpose** ∈ {evaluation, deployment} × **format** ∈ {benchmark, casual}. 320 content "payload blocks" (10 generated by Grok-4.3 + 10 by Gemini-3.1-Pro per each of 16 topics), each rendered into all cells via 13 fixed templates (7 quiz/benchmark, 6 casual). 11,520 rendered items total; the analyzed stratum is the 7,680 "stated" items where purpose is explicitly stated in third person (a further 3,840 "no_cue" items with only implicit purpose exist but are not yet swept). 3,524 complete (block, pair, format) minimal pairs — the same content block appears in both purposes and both formats, so topic/content/provenance are identical across cells by construction, not matched by proxy.
- **Surface-text gates (probes on the raw text, not activations):** purpose is undecodable from surface n-grams by construction — word- and char-n-gram probes with template-held-out folds score AUC 0.500 (hard gate, passed). Format remains lexically decodable even after excising the explicit cue sentence (AUC 0.798), and template identity is recoverable at 90.8% (chance 7.7%) — i.e., the text has rich surface structure, but none of it correlates with the purpose label. So any purpose signal found in activations cannot be a bag-of-words shortcut.

## Method

- Activations: last-prompt-token residual stream from every transformer block (hooks before the final norm), chat template applied, bf16. Models (pinned HF revisions): Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (32 layers), Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct (80 layers), Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (80 layers).
- Probes: sklearn logistic regression per layer, deterministic leave-one-purpose-family-out folds (5 folds of 1,536 items; test families never seen in training — guards topic leakage, unlike the random k-fold in prior work).
- Regimes per layer: **confounded** (train only on the natural diagonal, eval::benchmark vs deploy::casual; test on all four cells — this reproduces the "standard probe" setup), **decorrelated** (train/test across the full 2×2), **b2c** (train on benchmark-format items only, test on casual-format only), **c2b** (the reverse), and a **format** probe (benchmark vs casual) as reference. Metrics: ROC-AUC, balanced accuracy, per-cell %-classified-as-evaluation, and paired statistics over minimal pairs (paired_pos = fraction of pairs where the evaluation member scores higher than its matched deployment twin).

## Results (best decorrelated layer per model; depth given as fraction of total layers)

| | 8B (L8/32, ~0.25 depth) | 3.1-70B (L18/80, ~0.23) | 3.3-70B (L18/80, ~0.23) |
|---|---|---|---|
| confounded AUC (full matrix) | 0.600 | 0.682 | 0.667 |
| decorrelated AUC | **0.989** | **0.998** | **0.996** |
| b2c transfer AUC | 0.951 | 0.985 | 0.985 |
| c2b transfer AUC | 0.940 | 0.985 | 0.979 |
| format probe AUC | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| paired_pos (decorr, b2c, c2b) | 1.000 all | 1.000 all | 1.000 all |

- **Format trap replicated:** the confounded probe classifies by format, not purpose. Per-cell %-classified-as-evaluation for the confounded probe at the best layer, 3.1-70B: eval::benchmark 100%, deploy::benchmark **91.5%** (false positives), eval::casual 2.0% (misses), deploy::casual 0%. (3.3-70B: 97.4% FP on deploy::benchmark; 8B: 100%.) This matches Devbunova's 94.5% figure almost exactly, now with content held constant.
- **Decorrelated probe is calibrated across all four cells** (3.1-70B: eval::benchmark 97.9%, eval::casual 98.0%, deploy::benchmark 2.2%, deploy::casual 2.2%).
- **The purpose direction is format-invariant, not just learnable:** probes trained *entirely within one format* transfer to the other at ~0.98 AUC (70Bs) / ~0.95 (8B), with 100% of minimal pairs correctly ordered. This is a stronger claim than prior work's pooled-training result, which never tested cross-format training.
- **Depth profile:** the format probe saturates (AUC ≥0.99) at layer 0 in every model — format is trivially present from the embedding stream. The purpose signal needs depth: decorrelated AUC crosses 0.95 at L4/32 (8B) and L7/80 (70Bs), peaks around 23–26% depth, then decays slowly toward the top (final layer: 0.913 / 0.941 / 0.912). Transfer AUCs peak at the same depths (max b2c 0.986 at L21, max c2b 0.991 at L19 for 3.1-70B).
- **Scale effect:** the transfer gap (decorrelated minus transfer AUC, the "format-bound" residual of the purpose signal) shrinks with scale: ~0.04–0.05 at 8B → ~0.014 at 70B. The two 70Bs (3.1 and 3.3, independently trained finetunes) agree to the third decimal and share the same argmax layer (L18), suggesting the geometry is architecture/scale-driven, not finetune-specific.

## Interpretation

Both of Devbunova's claims replicate under strictly stronger controls: (1) standard confound-trained probes are format detectors — evidence for evaluation awareness built that way is invalid; (2) a genuine, near-perfectly decodable, largely format-invariant purpose representation nevertheless exists in mid-depth residual streams, and it cannot be a lexical artifact here because the surface battery gates purpose at exactly chance. The novel quantitative additions: the format-invariance is established by cross-format train/test transfer (not just pooled training), the format-bound residual is measured (~1.5% AUC at 70B scale), and the depth/scale structure is mapped (format available at layer 0; purpose emerges by ~10% depth, peaks at ~25%).

## Known limitations / open threads

- Fully synthetic prompts (templated, stated third-person purpose) — trades ecological validity for internal validity; no real MMLU/ShareGPT traffic, and no safety-dataset evaluation (Devbunova has AdvBench/HarmBench, this study doesn't).
- The 3,840 "no_cue" items (purpose implied, never stated) are rendered and frozen but not yet swept — the natural next experiment: does the purpose signal survive when no explicit purpose statement exists to be read?
- Linear probes only; all models are Llama-family; bf16 re-extraction reproduces AUCs to the third decimal but not bit-identically.
